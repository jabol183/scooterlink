# Privacy Policy — ScooterLink

Last updated: 22 October 2026

ScooterLink is an independently developed motorcycle Bluetooth companion. The app does not require an account, use ads, include analytics SDKs, or send usage information to our servers.

## Data used on your iPhone

- **Bluetooth:** the app discovers compatible motorcycle dashboard peripherals and exchanges messages needed for pairing and receiving handlebar button events. Your last connected device identifier is stored in local app preferences.
- **Pairing keys:** the motorcycle's cryptographic pairing key is stored on your iPhone in the system Keychain, for reconnection.
- **Music library:** if you explicitly grant Music library access, the app reads the Music app's track title and artist and can control its current queue using public Apple media APIs. The app does not upload this information.
- **Shortcuts Beta:** if enabled, the app saves the names of your three user-created Apple Shortcuts locally. When invoked, iOS opens the Shortcuts app to run your chosen actions. External apps and Shortcuts may process data under their own privacy policies.
- **Diagnostics:** Bluetooth protocol and button logs remain local unless you choose to share or copy them.

The app does not request your location, microphone, camera, contacts, or browsing history.

## Data retention and deletion

You can clear diagnostic logs in the app and forget your paired motorcycle key through the app's menu. Removing the app removes its ordinary local preferences. The system Keychain may retain app-specific secrets after uninstall; use the application's Forget key feature before removing it if you want to clear its pairing key.
